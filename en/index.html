Objections

Before you change the ad destination URL

You can see my offer page. What stops you from copying my offer?
We can — and it cannot be otherwise: deciding on the server which page goes out requires both pages to be registered in the dashboard. No server-side cloaker works without that, and anyone who says they route without seeing your page is describing a script on the page, not a server cloaker. What exists on our side is a named company: the platform is operated, billed and invoiced by BLACKINK TECNOLOGIA LTDA, CNPJ 66.362.785/0001-50, and what binds us is written in the Terms of Use — read them before you subscribe, not after.
Do I need to connect my ads account? Does it work outside Google?
No — no OAuth, no integration, no access to your account. And it works the same on Google Ads, Meta and TikTok because it depends on none of them: the decision is about who opened the URL, not which dashboard the click came from.
Will the ad point to one of your domains?
No. Two domains, both yours: one hosts the safe page and the offer page, the other is what goes in the ad. The BlackInk domain does not appear anywhere — not in the ad, not to Google, Meta or TikTok, not to the visitor, not to your customer. You do not share footprint with any other advertiser: another client's banned domain does not touch yours. And if you cancel, both domains stay yours.
If BlackInk goes down, does my traffic stop?
Every click passes through here — that is what puts the decision on the server instead of in a script, and it would be dishonest to pretend that does not make you dependent on us. So the useful answer is not “it never goes down”: the emergency exit is yours. Both domains are yours, and disconnecting them from BlackInk to serve your pages directly does not depend on our dashboard or opening a ticket; it is the same move as canceling. And if the problem is on our side, the channel that does not depend on the dashboard is suporte@blackink.cloud.
I already use another cloaker. Do I have to pause the campaign to migrate?
No. The whole migration happens outside the campaign: you register the safe page and offer page in the dashboard (or use BlackInk Clone) and adjust the rules while your current cloaker keeps running traffic as usual. Nothing changes for the visitor until you swap the ad destination URL — that swap is the only moment the campaign feels the migration, and you choose when. You can start with one campaign, compare it with the ones still on the old cloaker, and move the rest later.
What if the reviewer discovers the cloaker?
There is no cloaker for them to discover. They open your destination URL and receive the safe page directly — HTTP 200, no redirect, no detection JavaScript. Nothing is left in their browser showing that a choice happened. In exchange, the safe page has to stand on its own: BlackInk decides who sees what; it does not make bad content pass review.
What if it blocks a real buyer?
It happens — every filter errs to one side, and this one errs toward closing. That is why every signal is your toggle: bot, VPN, datacenter, device and country you turn on and off, and a disabled signal never sends anyone to the safe page. When a divert looks wrong, it is in the decision log with IP, ISP, geo, device and the reason — you see which rule caught it and turn that rule off. That is why the 7 days are with real traffic: calibrating this only happens on your campaign, not on ours.
Cloaking goes against the networks' stated policy. So?
It does — and nobody honest will say otherwise. The decision happening on the server means nothing is left in the reviewer's browser; it does not mean, and nobody can promise, that your account will never be banned. Anyone who guarantees “account never banned again” is selling something else. What changes here is who receives your page. What stays yours is the risk of the operation, the offer and the safe page.
What if I want to cancel? Is there a fee or notice period?
There is no contract and no lock-in. You have a 7-day guarantee on the amount charged. After that you cancel whenever you want, with no fee and no notice — access continues until the end of the period already paid and the subscription does not renew. Before you close, disconnect from BlackInk any domains still routing traffic — they are yours and they stay yours.
Do I need technical knowledge?
No. The dashboard is visual: register your pages, turn filters on and configure geo and device with toggles. BlackInk Clone makes it even easier if you already have a finished page. If you get stuck, help on the first configuration is free and, after that, support is by ticket in the platform.
How long does setup take?
Cloaker setup takes about 10 minutes. Register the safe page and offer page in the platform, configure filtering rules by geo, device and ISP, connect your domain to BlackInk — and routing works immediately. The team offers free support on the first configuration.
How do I set up my pages?
Register the HTML or code of the safe page and the offer page directly in the BlackInk dashboard. Alternatively, use BlackInk Clone: a platform tool that copies the structure of a page that already exists and leaves it ready as a safe page or offer page, without you rebuilding the HTML by hand. Then configure the filtering rules and keep your domain as the ad destination — the platform decides which page to serve to each visitor.
